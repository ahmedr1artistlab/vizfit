1. Introduction & Mission

VizFit (package: com.raza.vizfit) is an advanced Android mobile application engineered to help consumers accurately identify, visualize, and choose the correct garment sizes across various global and regional clothing brands. Our mission is to eliminate sizing guesswork, minimize e-commerce clothing return rates, and offer an interactive virtual fitting experience directly on the user's smartphone.

We believe biometric measurements and personal photos are deeply private. Therefore, VizFit operates under a strict privacy-by-design, local-first computing architecture: live camera frames and body measurements are never transferred to external servers.

2. Overview of App Features & Options

VizFit provides the following core features and options, which have been designed in compliance with Google Play Developer Program policies and global data privacy regulations:

📐 CameraX Body Measurement Scan

Guided on-screen silhouette overlays that assist users in aligning their body to extract proportional metrics (chest, waist, hips, shoulders, height).

🤖 Computer Vision Sizing AI

An intelligent sizing engine that maps body proportions against brand-specific sizing charts (Zara, Nike, Levi's, H&M, Uniqlo, Mango, Ralph Lauren, Khaadi, Gul Ahmed, etc.) to output recommended sizes (S, M, L, XL).

🪞 AR Virtual Try-On Mirror

Real-time Augmented Reality overlay using your phone's camera to render clothing items, fabric drape, and wireframes onto your physical space.

👤 Personalized Brand Fit Profiles

Custom profile management enabling you to store brand-specific fit preferences (Slim, Regular, Relaxed, Oversized) and fine-tune sizing for repeat shopping.

⚖️ Multi-Brand Size Comparator

Simultaneous cross-comparison of size gradings across multiple fashion brands and regional sizing charts (US, UK, EU, Pakistani).

👗 Virtual Closet & Saved Looks

Local cataloging of virtual try-on history, sizing comfort scores, and drape quality ratings stored safely on your device.

3. Camera Access, CameraX & AR Fitting Mirror

VizFit requests runtime access to your device's Camera (android.permission.CAMERA). This permission is required only when accessing the following specific features:

  • Live Camera Measurement Scan: Displaying alignment guides (head, shoulder, waist, and hip indicators) to calibrate frame proportions for metric estimation.
  • Augmented Reality (AR) Virtual Dressing Mirror: Projecting virtual garments, contours, and silhouette drape onto live preview frames in real time.
Runtime Permission Control: VizFit never accesses your camera without your explicit consent. Camera permission is requested at runtime with a clear explanatory prompt. You have full freedom to decline or revoke camera permission at any time via Settings > Apps > VizFit > Permissions > Camera. If camera permission is withheld, you can still input measurements manually.

4. On-Device Processing & Zero Biometric Upload

We respect the confidentiality of your physical appearance and body imagery:

  • RAM-Only Video Frame Analysis: Live video frames streamed from CameraX are analyzed transiently in volatile device memory (RAM) to calculate bounding boxes and silhouette contours. Frames are discarded immediately after calculation.
  • No Cloud Biometric Storage: VizFit does NOT stream, record, or upload your live camera frames, photos, facial landmarks, or raw body imagery to any remote cloud servers.
  • Zero Face Recognition: The application does not perform biometric facial recognition or identity tracking.

5. Brand Fit Profiles & Local Room Database

To deliver accurate size predictions that adapt to your personal taste, VizFit allows you to create and manage 'Fit Profiles' for various brands. This data is stored strictly in your device's sandboxed Room database:

  • Physical metrics: Height (cm/in), Weight (kg/lbs), Chest, Waist, Hips, Shoulder width.
  • Fit preferences: Tight, Slim Fit, Regular Balanced, Relaxed, Oversized.
  • Brand customizations: Fine-tuned adjustment offsets for brands you shop from repeatedly.

This profile data stays on your device. It is never sold, shared with data brokers, or synced to third-party databases.

7. Data Security, Encryption & Sandboxing

We take rigorous precautions to protect your data:

  • Encrypted Local Storage: User measurements stored in the Room database are protected by Android app sandbox isolation and internal AES encryption keys.
  • Secure Networking: External network requests (such as retrieving brand sizing guides or loading product thumbnails) are strictly routed over encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3).
  • In-Memory Scan Purge: When you complete or exit a camera scan session, temporary frame buffers are immediately released and reclaimed by the garbage collector.

8. User Rights, Data Control & Deletion (GDPR & CCPA)

Under regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), users possess explicit rights concerning their data:

  • Right to Access: You can view all saved profile measurements, brand preferences, and closet records inside the Profile and Closet tabs at any time.
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit any body dimension or fit preference with real-time updates.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You can wipe all profile records, measurement histories, and cached preferences instantly by selecting "Reset / Clear All Data" in the Profile menu.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling VizFit automatically and permanently removes all local SQLite/Room database files from your device.
